The 722nd Cake Forks has come to completion.
I am very happy with how they turned out.

## More Models

### Grogs

Being a Slug's Lament warband the 722nd Cake Forks needed a second unit of Grogs,
but to keep things different they have shakoes instead of bearskins.

The mushroom is done by putting a ball of greenstuff on a small brass rod then painting with Corax White and spots of Agrax Earthshade.

![Grogs](/assets/images/turnip28/grogs2.jpg)

### Toadie Sapper

I was quite happy with the sapper model that came in the kit, so not much was needed to be done.

![Sapper](/assets/images/turnip28/turnip_sapper.jpg)

### Toff

Finally I worked on the Toff.
The leader of the 722nd Cake Forks needed a cake,
the Turnip world not having any cakes the Toff had to improvise and use his head instead
-- not the one on his shoulders but one forcefully donated.

I decided to add a bit of orange, this draws your eye to the most important character in the warband
but still fits due to the rusty parts of all the other models.

![Toff](/assets/images/turnip28/toff.jpg)

## Complete Warband

Now the warband is complete.
